Questions

Which apps can I import from?

TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest and Facebook, plus nearly any recipe website or blog.

What if a video has no written recipe?

We transcribe the audio and read the on-screen text to rebuild the recipe. You can always edit it afterwards.

Is my recipe box private?

Yes. Your recipes are only visible to you unless you choose to share a collection.

Can I cancel Pro anytime?

Yes, with one tap in settings. Your recipes are yours to keep.
